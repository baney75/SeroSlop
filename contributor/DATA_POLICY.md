# Data policy

Do not submit minors, faces or biometric material, intimate/private content, credentials, paywalled material, or anything whose rights or provenance are unclear. The contributor must personally know the label and have permission to submit the image for review.

The label is a review report, not proof of origin. Maintainers must independently review submissions, record the decision and source evidence, delete quarantined raw bytes after review, and keep lineage before any training use. Training admission is a separate maintainer decision.
