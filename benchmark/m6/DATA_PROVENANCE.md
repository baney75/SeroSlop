Candidate inventories are metadata-only until P6. Publisher and revision pins,
member/shard byte hashes, labels, and image facts must independently verify.
The source-card license claim is not upstream-rights clearance; no model is
claimed commercially rights-cleared and no source pixels are redistributed.

labeled-test X-AIGD member. Omni-Fake SET remains the real-negative/train basis
and Omni-Fake OOD remains evaluation-only. NTIRE, Treasure, FLUX.2 2M,
reconstructed-real X-AIGD, OpenFake, and optional RISE are excluded. Google
private distribution is not claimed by the search-thumbnail or provider-CDN
fixture names. Attribution remains an append-only source-lock obligation.
